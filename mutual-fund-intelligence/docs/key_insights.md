# Key EDA Insights - [Flexi Cap Mutual Funds]

Within the Flexi Cap category, the 5-year CAGR ranges from 5.17% to 17.50%. Despite belonging to the same category, funds exhibit significantly different returns due to variations in portfolio allocation, stock selection, sector exposure, and fund management strategies.

Correlation between expense ratio and 5-year return: 0.26. This indicates a weak positive relationship, suggesting that a higher expense ratio does not necessarily result in better returns. Expense ratio alone is not a reliable predictor of fund performance.

360 ONE Mutual Fund delivered the highest average 3-year Sharpe Ratio (0.75) among the selected AMCs, indicating the strongest risk-adjusted performance in this sample.

During the COVID-19 market crash (Feb–Mar 2020), CANARA ROBECO FLEXICAP FUND – DIRECT PLAN – IDCW recorded the smallest drawdown of approximately -30.28%, compared with the sample average drawdown of -33.47%, making it the most resilient fund in the dataset.

CANARA ROBECO FLEXICAP FUND – REGULAR PLAN – GROWTH experienced the deepest historical maximum drawdown (-100%) in the dataset. This should be verified, as a -100% drawdown is unrealistic for an actively managed mutual fund and likely indicates missing or incomplete historical NAV data rather than actual performance.

Additional Pattern: Most funds are clustered around 14%–17% annual volatility and 9%–13% 5-year CAGR, indicating similar risk levels but noticeable variation in returns. A few outliers, such as certain Canara Robeco entries with unusually high volatility (~30%), stand apart from the rest and may warrant further investigation for data quality or exceptional market behavior.
